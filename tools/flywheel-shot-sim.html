What the model assumes

  • The ball rolls out between the two wheels, so it leaves at the average of their surface speeds times the grip efficiency, and spins at (back − front surface speed) ÷ ball diameter.
  • The front wheel is driven by the flywheel motor, so motor RPM = front wheel RPM × the motor ratio. That's the number Flywheel.setRPM takes.
  • The ball is a sphere with constant drag and constant spin. Lift coefficient = 1 / (2 + 1/S), where S = spin ratio.
  • Goal height, ball mass and efficiency are starting guesses. Measure your own and calibrate with a shot that scores.
